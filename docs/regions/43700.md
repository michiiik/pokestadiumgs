### Automatic rotation

`func_80043500` calls this updater after the idle countdown expires. It
increases the signed step at preview offset 0x0E by 16 while below 256,
adds that step to the display object's rotationY at offset 0x20, and damps
rotationX at 0x1E toward zero with `Math_StepToS`. The damping increment is
bounded by the current step and by `max(abs(rotationX) / 12, 8)`.

Eight initial probes reach matching opcode/register sequences but leave
the real rotation-pointer spill at stack offset 28 rather than 24. A second
eight-cell family removes the separate absolute-value temporary, varies
the actual pointer/declaration lifetime and tests which consumed step is
capped. Declaring the two signed halfword scalars before the rotation
pointer and computing the absolute-value quotient directly matches all
176 bytes. No fake local, stack home, dummy expression, helper-signature
change, padding or instruction patch is needed.
The first accepted whole-TU object is frozen privately with SHA256
`84afd5dbd68e572cfabdc0ce9d37108ec182939c71ec0ed40a975efb66f792dc`.
